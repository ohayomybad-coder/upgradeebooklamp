Whispers Beyond Rain

Written by Aether Vale

In a city where the rain never stops, forgotten memories begin returning through voices hidden inside storms.

Chapter 1

The City That Never Slept

Rain crawled slowly across the shattered windows of the apartment. The city lights below flickered through the fog like fading memories.

Kael sat silently near the balcony, listening to the endless storm.

Somewhere between the thunder and the distant train sirens, he thought he heard someone whisper his name.

The streets below were empty. Neon reflections trembled in puddles across the dark pavement.
